Session Overview

Three functional practices for the defending unit, run back to back. Each one narrows the same problem from a different angle: holding shape against an attacking unit, squeezing in a tight three-zone area, and surviving a numerical disadvantage.

The areas shrink and then widen again across the session — 50 × 40, down to 30 × 35, back out to 50 × 45 — so plan the field markings before you start.

The Three Phases
Part 1

Defensive Compactness

343 unit preventing penetration in the final third

06-P9 · Function

  • Area 50 × 40 yds
  • Shape 343 defensive unit
  • Numbers Unit v 4 attackers + pivot

Organisation

  • Functional practice built around a 343 defensive unit, focused on preventing penetration passes in the final third — especially against an attacking group of four.
  • A pivot player sits behind the attacking unit to start play, letting the attackers vary their angles of approach and exploit space.
  • Attackers challenge the unit with runs onto balls played in behind, plus short, quick combinations to test organisation and force mistakes.

Key coaching points

  • Maintain compact positioning to prevent penetration through central areas.
  • Communicate effectively between units to cover and support each other.
  • Recognise when to press individually and when to press as a coordinated group.
Part 2

Defensive Pressure — Back 3

Three zones, 3v2 attacks, finish in the mini goals

10-P13 · Advanced Lead

  • Area 30 × 35 yds, three zones
  • Shape Back three
  • Numbers 3 defenders v 2 + 1 support

Organisation

  • A 30 × 35-yard area focused on developing defensive shape when set up with a back three.
  • Three zones: three defenders in one 10-yard zone, two attackers in the central 15-yard zone, one supporting attacker in the other 10-yard zone.
  • The supporting attacker passes into the central zone to trigger a 3v2 attack.
  • Defenders aim to win possession and finish in either mini goal. Attackers can only use the supporting player twice in an attack before going alone.
  • Switch out one defender and all three attackers after every two attacks.

Key coaching points

  • Stay compact and squeeze the attackers.
  • High levels of communication when pressing and covering.
  • Shift across quickly to close the gaps.
  • Recognise when to apply a high press — know the pressing triggers.
  • Patience in possession: draw the attackers in to play out.
  • Attackers must recognise gaps and penetrate quickly.
Part 3

Defending Outnumbered

6v5 — force play wide and keep the ball in front

13-P9 · Function

  • Area 50 × 45 yds, two zones
  • Shape 32 from a 343 base
  • Numbers 6 attackers (433) v our 5

Organisation

  • Focused on the defensive and central midfield unit defending compact when outnumbered.
  • In a 50 × 45-yard area, six attacking players set up in a 33 (433 base) against our 32 (343 base).
  • The objective is for the compact defending five to force play away from goal into wide areas and keep the ball in front of them.
  • When they regain it, they finish in one of two mini goals at the opposite end.

Key coaching points

  • Keep the distances between the back three and the two central midfielders correct — compactness and defensive balance.
  • Central midfielders block passes into the opposition’s number 9, cutting off the key channels.
  • Always force play out wide, directing the ball away from the central goal area to reduce scoring chances.
